Our Story

It all started with a simple idea—bringing warmth, comfort, and a little magic into everyday moments. In the heart of our cozy home studio, surrounded by the gentle flicker of candlelight, we poured our very first batch of scented candles. What began as a personal escape—an expression of creativity and a love for beautiful aromas—quickly grew into a passion we wanted to share with the world. Every candle we create is hand-poured in small batches using eco-friendly soy wax, premium fragrance oils, and a whole lot of love. We believe in the power of scent to transport, soothe, and inspire. Whether it’s the calming notes of lavender after a long day or the nostalgic hint of vanilla that reminds you of home, our mission is to bottle those emotions and moments into something tangible. This little business is more than just candles—it’s about connection, self-care, and making life’s simple pleasures a little more special. Thank you for being here and letting us be a part of your story, one candle at a time.🪷 Welcome to our world. Light up, slow down, and breathe it in.💕

At CANDALERIAA, we believe in crafting moments of peace and joy through the magic of scent. Our candles are hand-poured with love, using natural wax and high-quality essential oils.

Each fragrance tells a story — of dreamy beaches, misty forests, warm vanilla evenings, and tropical bliss.
